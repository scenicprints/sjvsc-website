Swiss wrestling

Schwingen

The national sport of Switzerland. The club has wrestled since 1926 and holds three tournaments a year.

Three a year

Tournaments

The club holds three Schwingfests a year under the West Coast Swiss Wrestling Association. Dates for the year ahead are set each December.

TournamentNextWho wrestles
Spring SchwingfestSat 5 June 2027Seniors and juniors
Buebe & Meitli SchwingfestSat 24 July 2027Children up to 15
Championship SchwingfestSat 4 September 2027Seniors and juniors, 16 and over

All dates are on the events calendar.

Practice

Thursday evenings at six, at the hall. Beginners and children are welcome and the club has breeches to lend. Call Don Widmer on (209) 915-4245 before your first night.

Come and watch. Admission to a Schwingfest is $20 for anyone 15 and over. Wrestling starts late morning and runs most of the day.
