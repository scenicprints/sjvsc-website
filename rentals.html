Rent the hall

The club rents the hall and the grounds for weddings, parties, memorials and meetings.

The room

The hall has a dance floor, a built-in wooden bar, and the cantonal crests mounted along the wall above it.

The kitchen has a stainless steel prep island.

The grounds can be rented with the hall or on their own, and there is a playground. Dinner outside and dancing inside is a common arrangement.

Capacity, tables, chairs, access times
