# The hall rental site, kept

The club ran a **second** website. Not the Network Solutions one in
`../old-site/`, but a separate GoDaddy site at
`sjvalleyswissclub.godaddysites.com`, built in 2023 and devoted entirely to
renting the hall. The old main site's Hall Rental page pointed at it —
"New website for Hall Rentals, Member and Non-Member Applications, FAQ's and
more information" — and by the time that page was audited in October 2026,
the link was already dead.

It is gone now. Every address on it returns 404, including the front page.
This folder is what could be got back, taken on **16 November 2026**.

- `documents/` — **the five rental PDFs, and the reason this folder exists.**
- `pages/` — the words from all nine pages.

## The documents are the point

Everything else on that site the new website already has, and in more detail.
The money matches to the dollar: $600 for a member, $1,200 for a non-member,
$500 to clean, $800 held as a deposit, the two-year membership rule, $1M of
insurance, two security guards up to 199 guests. The new site adds what that
one never said — cancellation terms, access hours, the minimum age.

The paperwork is the exception. Five PDFs on club letterhead, naming
San Joaquin Valley Swiss Club, Inc., 110 N. Manley Rd., P.O. Box 373:

| File | Pages |
|---|---|
| `member-application-2023.pdf` | 1 |
| `member-rental-agreement-2023.pdf` | 2 |
| `non-member-rental-application.pdf` | 1 |
| `non-member-rental-agreement.pdf` | 3 |
| `waiver-of-liability-hold-harmless.pdf` | 1 |

These are the documents a renter actually signs. The new rentals page works
out what a booking costs and states the rules, but offers nothing to
download, so until these go up the club has a calculator and no contract.

## How they were nearly lost

The Wayback Machine archived the nine pages and **none** of the downloads.
It saves what a page says, not what a page offers, and these were offered
rather than said.

What saved them is that GoDaddy serves uploads from `img1.wsimg.com`, a file
store with its own lifetime. The site is 404 and the files behind it still
answer. So they were pulled from the original host, at full size, and are
here — not re-created from an archive, but the files themselves.

That will not last forever. It was luck, and it is the kind of luck worth
not relying on twice.

## What is not here

- **Every photograph.** The Hall, The Grounds and Past Events were picture
  pages, and the pictures came from the same `img1.wsimg.com` store — but
  unlike the PDFs, nothing recorded their addresses. The Wayback Machine
  captured the pages without them, so there is no list to work from. The
  pictures are gone.
- **The three F.A.Q. answers.** The questions survive; the answers sat in a
  collapsible panel the archive never opened. See `pages/faq.txt`.
- **Whatever was behind "Online Bill Pay".** Both rate pages carried the
  link. It is not archived and does not resolve.

## Two things it turned up

`SJValleySwissClub@gmail.com` was the contact address on it — a club account
nobody has mentioned, separate from `sjvscwebmaster@gmail.com`.

And if the club still has a GoDaddy account, it may still have a bill. The
site being down usually means the plan lapsed, but a card on file renews
quietly. That is the Network Solutions lesson, and it is worth one look.
