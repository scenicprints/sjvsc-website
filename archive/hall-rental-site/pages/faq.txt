Frequently Asked Questions

Please reach us at SJValleySwissClub@gmail.com if you cannot find an answer
to your question.

- What is the maximum guest count?
- Can we come in early to set up and decorate?
- Is a no-host bar allowed?

[NOTE: the three answers are lost. They sat in a collapsible accordion whose
 contents GoDaddy loaded separately, and the Wayback Machine captured the
 questions without them. Clicking each question in the archived page opens
 nothing. The answers are not recoverable from this source.

 Two of the three the new site does answer, from the rental agreements:
 setting up the day before runs 9am to 8pm on request, and the hall is for
 private parties with no beverages sold, which rules a no-host bar out.

 The maximum guest count it does not answer, and neither does any other
 document the club has. The only number anywhere is in the security guard
 rule -- four guards for up to 399 attendees -- which sets a floor, not a
 capacity. That question is now in docs/ask-the-club.md.]
