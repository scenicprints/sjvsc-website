Member Investment is $600.

Please note that Members are required to have held membership for a minimum
of 24 months and to be in good club standing in order to receive this rate.

All events, both Member and Non-Member will have full access to Club
amenities such as tables and chairs, a large kitchen and walk-in
refrigerator.

- Application due at time of request.
- Rental fee ($600) due one month prior to event.
- Cleaning fee ($500) due 10 days prior to event.
- Certificate of insurance, naming SJVSC as additionally insured, in the
  amount of $1M due 10 days prior to event. Additional coverage for
  Alcohol/Liquor Liability must be attached if alcohol is served.
  Certificates of Insurance must be furnished 10 days prior to use of the
  facility.
- Security contract OR Hold Harmless agreement due 10 days prior to event.

Funeral and Celebration of Life fees are waived for members.

Required Documents
- MEMBER APPLICATION (pdf)
- MEMBER RENTAL AGREEMENT (pdf)
- WAIVER OF LIABILITY (pdf)
