------------------------------------------------------------
Non-Member investment is $1200.

All events, both Member and Non-Member will have full access to Club
amenities such as tables and chairs, a large kitchen and walk-in
refrigerator.

- Application and Security Deposit ($800) are due at time of request. Please
  note, the security deposit is fully refundable if facility is left clean
  and free of breakage.
- Rental fee ($1,200) due one month prior to event.
- Cleaning Fee ($500) due 10 days prior to event.
- Certificate of insurance, naming SJVSC as additionally insured, in the
  amount of $1M due 10 days prior to event. Additional coverage for
  Alcohol/Liquor Liability must be attached if alcohol is served.
  Certificates of Insurance must be furnished 10 days prior to use of the
  facility.
- Security guard contract due 10 days prior to event. Security guards are
  required for all Non-Member events. 2 guards for up to 199 attendees,
  3 guards for up to 299 and 4 guards for up to 399.

Required Documents
- NON-MEMBER RENTAL APPLICATION (pdf)
- NON-MEMBER RENTAL AGREEMENT (pdf)
- WAIVER OF LIABILITY (pdf)
