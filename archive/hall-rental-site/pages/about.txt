Who We Are

The San Joaquin Valley Swiss Club is owned and operated by the Swiss Club
Members, a non-profit ethnic organization set up and supported by the Swiss
American Community in the Central Valley.

Located on N. Manley Road in Ripon, California. Sitting behind a private
gate, on nearly 6 acres of lush landscape and tranquil settings.

Planning a wedding or your next big birthday? Whatever it may be, let us
help! Our facilities are available for rent to both Members and Non-Members.
