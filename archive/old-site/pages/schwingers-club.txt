SCHWINGERS CLUB
Schwinger Club -- Annual Schwinger Dinner

The Schwinger Club is an auxillary of the SJVSC and helps promote the Schwingen as a cultural heritage that those who came from Switzerland brought the sport to America.

The Schwinger club donates prizes and money to the Schwingfests held bi-annually at the club.

Each year a Schwinger Dinner is put on by the Schwinger Club to a sold out crowd. This typically takes place the 2nd Saturday in March. About 300 people are in attendance for the Prime RIb, Shrimp and Alplermagaronen Dinner. There are raffle prizes and a silent auction dessert as well as the famed Gun Auction.

We thank everyone for their generosity and support ! Tickets go on sale in January and sell out fast! Get your tickets early !

WHAT IS SCHWINGEN ?

Schwingen (from German schwingen "to swing"), also known as Swiss wrestling (French lutte Suisse) and natively (and colloquially) as Hoselupf (Swiss German for "breeches-lifting"), is a style of folk wrestling native to Switzerland, more specifically the pre-alpine parts of German-speaking Switzerland. Wrestlers wear Schwingerhosen ("wrestling breeches") with belts that are used for taking holds. Throws and trips are common because the first person to pin his/her opponent's shoulders to the ground wins the bout.

Schwingen is considered a "national sport" of Switzerland, alongside Hornussen and Steinstossen. Schwingen and Steinstossen were included as Nationalturnen ("national gymnastics") in the Eidgenoessisches Turnfest at Lausanne in 1855.

The modern history of organized Schwingen tournaments begins with the Unspunnenfest of 1805.

West Coast Swiss Wrestling Association

The West Coast Swiss Wrestling Association (WCSWA) was founded in 1957 to unite Swiss Wrestling clubs and associated members of the West Coast for the purpose of maintaining and advancing the fundamentals and techniques of Swiss Wrestling. Contact your local club to get started.

Each year our member clubs host 7 of the 10 annual Schwingfests here on the West Coast of the USA that stretch from San Deigo, CA to Tacoma, WA. Our first Schwingfest each year is the Spring in Holtville, CA The annual Championship Schwingfest rotates between our member clubs. In 2023 it will be July 15 in Truckee, CA. Visiting wrestlers from Switzerland are always welcome. Schwing Klubs who want to travel on the West Coast can visit 2-3 Schwingfests depending upon the time of year. Contact us for more information.
