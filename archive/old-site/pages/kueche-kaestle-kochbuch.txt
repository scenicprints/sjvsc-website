New Kueche Kaestli Kochbuch
*Kitchen Kupboard Kookbook

COMING SOON. . . .

The SJVSC Kueche Kaestli Ladies previously had 2 Cookbooks that were produced by with recipes from Swiss Ladies all up and down the West Coast with their favorite recipes. The first book was a white cover and the 2nd book was a red cover. We are creating a new one with lots of favorite recipes from the old cookbooks and many new recipies. This will be for sale at our 2026 Fall Schwingfest Labor Day Weekend. More info to follow!

BRAETZLI
Find this delectable homemade Swiss Cookie made, tried and tested by the generations of Swiss in our club

SCHNITZEL
Another favorite from different Cantons.

LINZERTORTE
Many different varieties and methods making this scrumptious pastry - shared from so many kitchens and generations. This is also a favorite treat that can be purchased many times at the Dessert Auction at the Schwinger Dinner.

Aelpler Magarona
A favorite of anyone attending the Swiss Club. This could also be on the menu at a Fall Schwingfest and is a favorite at the Schwinger Dinner. Made with Potato's and Swiss Cheese topped with crispy onions.

CHEESE FONDUE
While EMMI makes a great prepackaged Fondue - you can't go wrong incorporating your own Gruyere, Appenzell or Raclette Cheese into a recipe.

Buender NussTorte
Another Walnut Pie that will be sure to impress your guests.
