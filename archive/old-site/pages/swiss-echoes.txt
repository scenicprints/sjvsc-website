Swiss Echoes of the Alps

Dear Friends,

The Swiss Echoes of the Alps Concert on April 11th will be one everyone will be talking about for along time. Please see the vidoes of the event below. More than 75 singers from across the country joined the Swiss Echoes in a spectacular musical presentation. The evening showcased beloved Swiss traditions, including Alphorn players, yodelers and inspiring musician presentations.
Following the concert, guests enjoyed a Swiss-inspired dinner and dance to live Swiss music by the Haslital Kapelle, making this a truly memorable cultural celebration.

We enjoyed celebrating this remarkable Swiss musical gathering with you.

The San Joaquin Valley "Swiss Echoes" was founded in 1947 when a group of 28 singers joined as charter members with Mr. John Meyer as Music Director and first President. The new chorus became members of the United Swiss Singing Societies of the Pacific Coast (USSSPC) and participated in their first Saengerfest in Los Angeles in 1948. The "Swiss Echoes" have taken part in every Saengerfest since then.

The highlights of the "Swiss Echoes" were inhosting the first California Swiss Singing & Jodeling Festival in 1956, the 10th Saengerfest in 1960 and the 16th Saengerfest in 1978. All were coordinated by Saengervater Ken Hafer with Music Director Bertha Davis. The 24th Saengerfest in 2002 was presided over by Saengervater Fred Kaufmann with Music Director Heidi Brunk. Trudi Boesch was the accompanist for all three Saengerfests. In 2017, the "Swiss Echoes" will be hosting the 29th Saengerfest which will be presided over by USSSPC President Heidi Betschart.

Currently, the 32 member mixed chorus is directed by Agnes Litfin-Olmsted of Stockton with accompanist SaraRae Vanderhelm of Modesto. The "Swiss Echoes" present an Annual Spring and Fall Concert singing melodies from their native homeland, Switzerland, dressed in colorful costumes of Swiss tradition.

New members are always welcome. Practice night is Tuesday evening from 7:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m. at the San Joaquin Valley Swiss Club Hall, East Main Street & Manley Road, Ripon California.

For more information about joining the "Swiss Echoes" please contact: SJVSwissEchoes@gmail.com
