90th Birthday Party

We were excited to celebrate 90yr+ members on May 4th 2025, who turned 90 in 2023, 2024 and 2025. Out of the 13 members we were celebrating, 12 attended! Paula Ott, Hannah Sigrist Kessler, Fred Kaufman, Neil Van Duyn, Pearl Sieler, Camile Domine, Heidi Ambiel, Jim Duarte, Walter Gemperle, Joyce Moser Tsuchiyama, Marian Goncalves, and Ernie Scheuber. We are still hearing what a special day it was for ALL who attended, and when's the next 90th birthday party! It's worked out to celebrate every 3 years but it could be earlier than 2028!!

Countless thanks to all who helped make the day special. Don Widmer, for being our excellent MC and a positive force about the history and continuation of our Club! Heidi & Marge Betschart who outdid themselves with the decorations, programs and appetizers! Arthur Brogli for entertaining us with his wonderful accordion and singing! Jennifer Hinkle for greeting our Honorees with crowns, badges and wristlets as they entered the Hall. Erika Betschart for taking pictures of all the birthday "Honorees"! Karen Aufdermaur-Berghorst and her crew for providing us with a delicious lunch and so many yummy desserts. Lots of appetizers to start off the day and then lasagna courtesy of Karen and Angie Parham was served with green salad, fruit salad and garlic bread and they were delicious and gone by the end of the event. Seven membership applications were given out to prospective members, and five have since become members!

We REALLY appreciate these senior members ... they've helped make our Club what it is today! If you've never been to this celebration, it's time to enjoy a Sunday afternoon with some of your senior Swiss friends!!

AGAIN ... THANKS TO ALL WHO HELPED MAKE THIS A SUCCESSFUL EVENT...AGAIN!!

CATHY QUINN, Membership Officer
