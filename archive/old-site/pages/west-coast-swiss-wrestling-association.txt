IMPERIAL VALLEY -- Next: October 16th 2027
Enjoy their 105TH Schwingfest in Holtville, CA. Kick off the year with some great Schwingen from up and down the Valley. This also starts the Perpetual Trophy Award for 2026 and the George E Morris Award for 2026

LEWIS PACIFIC - FRANCES, WA -- July 2-4th 2027
The Frances Schwingfest is always the 4th of July Weekend - tucked back into the trees this is a weekend of Schwingen, Music and Fun. Seniors Wrestle on Saturday, Buebe on Sunday

TACOMA SWISS SPORTSMAN -- Saturday June TBD and August 8
Tacoma Swiss Sportsman Club in Bonney Lake, WA offers 2 Schwingfests. One in June and one in August. This club provides a great Swiss backdrop feeling of Switzerland. We see lots of competition here.

SAN DIEGO COUNTY SWISS -- Saturday, June 20th 2026
Surrounded in the beautiful San Diego area of Chula Vista this hall offers a great schwingfest day with lots of entertainment and things to do for the kids.

SIERRA SWISS CLUB -- Saturday, July 18 2026
A favorite Schwingfest for everyone in the mountain town of Truckee, CA. This is held at the Truckee Regional Center each year amongst the tall trees its almost like being in Switzerland.

AELPLER GRUPPE - NEWARK -- Saturday, August 29th 2026
Nestled amongst the tall trees and the growing City of Newark this Schwingfest offers the best of both and the hall of generations of Schwingers! Great food and wonderful atmosphere.

2025 SCHWINGEN RULES UPDATE

DURING THE WINTER OF 2025 A SUBCOMMITTEE OF ALL WCSWA CLUBS PARTICIPATED IN A RULES REVIEW FOR ALL WCSWA SCHWINGFESTS.

THE GOAL WAS TO STANDARDIZE THE RULES FOR EVERY SCHWINGFEST SO THAT SCHWINGERS, JUDGES, SPECTATORS AND SWISS VISITORS KNOW HOW THE MATCHES WILL BE JUDGED. THE RULES WERE ALSO UPDATED TO INCLUDE THE CURRENT JUDGING STANDARDS IN SWITZERLAND. THIS IS A LIVING DOCUMENT AND WILL BE REVIEWED AT THE END OF EACH SEASON.
