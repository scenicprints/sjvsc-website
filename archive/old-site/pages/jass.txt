Jass - Get ready to test your hand at Jass -- Hall Opens at 12:00 - if you don't know how to play we have a learning table!

Need to learn how to play? There are many apps available that you can play on your phone. Some are with Jass Cards and some of the rules are a bit different than playing at the Swiss Club. Just check out Swiss Jass Apps

Download the Cheat Sheet on how to play American Jass. It can be played 4 or 6 handed. This is just to get you started more Jass enthusiasts have a greater knowledge and skill set that they bring to the card table! Sometimes a little more aggressive as well! But Jass at the Swiss Club is the gentler version!

Jass at the Swiss Club - we play Men on one side - Women on the other - after one hand one side of the table moves down one space - which makes changing partners easy. This happens after each round. We play nine hands before lunch, after lunch the opposite side moves. You get to visit and play with new people each hand. Makes it for a very enjoyable day!

A lite lunch is served between rounds and we have a raffle and door prizes. Download the .pdf for more information on how the play works at the Swiss Club!
