# The old site, kept

A copy of what stood at `sjvscriponswiss.com` before the club moved, taken on
**13 November 2026** while the old site was still serving. It is here so that
cancelling the Network Solutions plan destroys nothing.

- `images/` — 32 photographs, 6.4 MB. Named `<builder-folder>__<original name>`.
- `documents/` — the 3 PDFs the site linked to, 6.8 MB.
- `pages.txt` — all 24 pages and which pictures were on each.
- `image-ids.txt` — the builder paths the pictures came from.

## What was taken, and how

The old site was built with Network Solutions' Website Builder (Webzai). It
renders entirely in JavaScript: the HTML served to a browser or a search engine
contains the page title and nothing else — no headings, no body text, not one
link. That is worth recording on its own, because it means the club's site has
been effectively invisible to Google for as long as it has existed. The new
site is plain HTML and is not.

So the pages had to be opened in a real browser and read after rendering. The
pictures were then pulled from the builder's storage at full size: most were
being served as `_d400` or `_d600` thumbnails, and the originals were still
there behind them, which is why `Alphorns_Grindelwald.jpg` is 702 KB here and
was 40 KB on the page.

## What is not here

- **One photograph is lost.** `16184736_lel_600.jpg`, on the scholarships page,
  404s at the builder's storage under every path it is referenced by. It was
  already broken on the live site.
- **The text.** Not copied, because it is not at risk: the new site was written
  from it, and `docs/current-site-audit.md` records the rest — the board roster,
  the mission statement, the membership tiers, the Swiss Echoes history, the
  2026 calendar and the copy errors.
- **A browsable replica.** These are the club's materials, not a working copy of
  the old site.

## The thing worth knowing

There was no photograph trove. `docs/current-site-audit.md` guessed that
`/playground`, `/swiss-miss`, `/may-birthday-celebration` and
`/eidgenössisches-2022` were galleries hiding the club's pictures. They are not:
between them they hold seven photographs, and nine of the twenty-four pages
carry none at all. Thirty-two pictures is the whole of what the club has had
online. The new site's thin bands will not be filled from here.
