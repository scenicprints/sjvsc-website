The flyer

One poster, up all year, changed when the next one arrives. It does not go on the home page itself — a short band does, cut from the poster, and clicking that opens the poster on a page of its own.

Sign in at the top of the page to change it.

The flyer

The newsletter or poster itself. A PDF of any number of pages, or a single picture.

The picture for the home page

The band on the home page is cut from a picture. A browser cannot take one out of a PDF, so if the flyer is a PDF, save its front page as a picture and choose it here. Leave it empty and the band appears without artwork, which is tidy but plainer.

What it is

The band comes off the home page the day after. Leave it empty and the flyer stands until it is replaced.
