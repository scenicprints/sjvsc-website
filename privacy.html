What this website does with information

Privacy

Short, because the site does very little. It is run by the San Joaquin Valley Swiss Club, a non-profit organisation in Ripon, California.

If you are just visiting

There is no account to make, no newsletter sign-up and no advertising. The site sets no cookies.

The site counts its visitors. When a page opens it tells the club’s own database which page it was, which page linked to it, whether it was a phone or a computer, and the country and state Cloudflare says the visit came from. Your network address is not kept: it goes into a code that changes every day and cannot be turned back into an address, and that code is how two visits on one day are known to be one person. The next day you are a new visitor. The board sees the totals — how many came, which pages, where from — and nothing about any one person. Nothing is given to anyone else.

The hall rental calculator is worked out inside your own browser. The date, the number of guests and the options you tick are never sent to the club or anywhere else; the site counts only that a quote was worked out. If you want to ask about a date, you contact the club yourself.

Two companies that see your visit

The site is served by Cloudflare, which keeps ordinary server records of requests, as any web host does.

The typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google receives the request for them, which includes your network address and browser. The club has no say in what Google does with that.

If you are on the board and sign in

Only invited board members can sign in. Sign-in is handled by Clerk, which stores the name and email address of each person who has an account, and keeps you signed in between visits. A browser that has signed in is taken to be a board member’s, and its visits to the public pages are no longer counted, so the board does not inflate its own numbers. The Visits screen shows this and can switch it off.

Those who sign in with Google give Clerk a name and email address from their Google account. The club asks Google for nothing else — no contacts, no calendar, no files, no access to the account beyond knowing who you are.

Every change made through the admin screens is recorded: what was changed, when, and the name of the person who changed it. That history is visible to the board and is kept so that changes to the club’s own website can be accounted for.

What the club does not do

Nothing here is sold, rented or shared. There is no advertising and no profiling. The club has no interest in anything beyond running its own website.

Asking about your information

If you are a board member and want your account or your name in the change history removed, ask and it will be done. For anything else about this page, write to the webmaster.

In short

  • VisitorsCounted, not followed. Which page, where from, phone or computer, country and state. No cookies, no address kept.
  • Rental calculatorRuns in your browser. Only the fact of a quote is counted.
  • Board sign-inName and email, held by Clerk
  • Change historyWho changed what, and when
  • Sold or sharedNever
