What the club charges, and what it asks a renter to produce. Everything the rate card and the quote show comes from here, so a price changed on this screen is a price changed on the website.

What the hall costs

One price for a member and one for everybody else. The same hall, whichever day of the week it is.

Fees

Charged on top of the rent. A fee marked returned is a deposit: it is held rather than spent, and the quote keeps it out of the total.

When the rent does not apply

A reason the hall is free. The fees above still apply.

What they are using

Inside, outside, or both. Put a price on one and the quote starts charging for it.

Also using

The kitchen, the stage, the pit, the bar. All free today. Put a number in and it appears on the next quote.
