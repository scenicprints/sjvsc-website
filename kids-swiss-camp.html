Kids Swiss Camp

Another great day of Swiss activities, making friends, and fun.

The day

The camp is a full day of Schwingen, Steinstossen, animal friends, baking and more, for children who have completed Kindergarten through 8th grade.

Places are limited to 60 children, and all campers receive a t-shirt.

Scholarships are available for those in need.

Applying

Each participant requires a separate application, returned before the camp. Forms must include contact information for parents and guardians, emergency contacts, shirt size, and relevant medical information including allergies, medications and food restrictions.

Parents give consent regarding media photography and participation in co-ed Schwingen, and authorise emergency medical treatment and transport for their child if necessary.

Sponsors

We would love to get each activity sponsored, for $100 or more. Your name or company will be on display as a thank you for your support.

Please reach out to Katharina or Regina Beeler at RiponKSC@gmail.com for any questions or sponsorships. Danke — Merci.
