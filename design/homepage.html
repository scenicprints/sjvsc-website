Switzerland's national sport

Schwingen, in a ring of sawdust

Two wrestlers take hold of each other's breeches and try to put the other flat on his back. No mat, no points for style — just sawdust, a grip, and the Schlussgang to finish the day. We have wrestled here since 1926, and the club still runs three Schwingfests a year under the West Coast Swiss Wrestling Association.

  • PracticeThursdays at six, at the hall. Call Don — (209) 915-4245
  • SpringSpring Schwingfest, the first Saturday in June
  • SummerBuebe & Meitli Schwingfest for the children, with the Members' BBQ
  • SeptemberChampionship Schwingfest — the big one, with Steinstossen

Since 1947

The Swiss Echoes

Thirty-two voices in Swiss costume, singing the melodies of home. The chorus has been to every Sängerfest of the United Swiss Singing Societies since 1948, and has hosted five of them. Spring and autumn concerts, with alphorns and yodelers.

Practice is Tuesday evenings, seven to nine. New singers always welcome.
