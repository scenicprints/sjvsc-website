Saturday 2 October 2027

Oktoberfest

Dinner, music and dancing at the hall.

The evening

Doors open in the late afternoon, dinner is served in the evening, and there is live music and dancing after.

It is one of the club's two big fundraising events of the year and it sells out most years.

Tickets

Tickets are sold in advance and the hall fills, so do not leave it to the week before.

Sponsors

Local businesses sponsor the evening. Ask the secretary for what the packages include.
