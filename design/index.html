Schwingen

Swiss wrestling, the national sport of Switzerland. The club holds three tournaments a year and practises Thursday evenings at the hall.

About the sport →

Hall rental

Room for about 300 at a sit-down dinner. Maple dance floor, bar, commercial kitchen and grounds outside. Members rent at a discount.

Membership

702 members. Dues are $50 a year. Meetings are the second Wednesday of the month at 7pm.

Purpose of the club

To unite and strengthen the social ties of the Swiss people, those of Swiss descent and their supporting friends; and to encourage, promote and preserve the culture and traditions of Switzerland.

From the club's constitution, 1926.
