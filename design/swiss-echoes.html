The club's chorus, founded 1947

Swiss Echoes

Thirty-two singers. Spring and autumn concerts in Swiss costume. Practice is Tuesday evenings at the hall.

The chorus

The Swiss Echoes began in 1947 with 28 charter members and John Meyer as director. The chorus joined the United Swiss Singing Societies of the Pacific Coast and sang at its first Sängerfest in Los Angeles in 1948. It has been to every one since.

The club has hosted the Sängerfest five times: 1956, 1960, 1978, 2002 and 2017. It also hosted the first California Swiss Singing and Jodeling Festival in 1956.

Today the chorus has 32 mixed voices. Agnes Litfin-Olmsted of Stockton directs, with SaraRae Vanderhelm of Modesto accompanying.

Concerts

Two a year, spring and autumn, sung in Swiss costume. The spring concert in April 2026 drew more than 75 singers from across the country, with alphorn players and yodelers, followed by a Swiss dinner and dancing to the Haslital Kapelle.

The chorus also sings at the Christmas party each December.

Singing with us

New singers are always welcome and you do not need to be Swiss or read music. Practice is Tuesday evenings from 7 to 9 at the hall. Come to one and see.

Write to SJVSwissEchoes@gmail.com, or call Chico Gunder, the chorus president, on (209) 322-5199.
