1926 – 2026

About the club

Founded in Ripon in 1926 and run by its members ever since.

How it started

The San Joaquin Valley Swiss Club was founded in Ripon in 1926 by Swiss families farming in the valley. It was incorporated in 1941.

The club is a non-profit organisation, owned and run by its members.

The hall

The hall sits on Manley Road at the edge of Ripon. The carved sign over the door gives both dates, 1926 and 1941. Inside, the cantonal crests are mounted along the wall above the dance floor.

The club uses it for its own events through the year and rents it out the rest of the time.

One hundred years

The club marked its hundredth year in 2026 with work on the building: a new entrance in place of the old 1940s door, the dance floor refinished, the kitchen remodelled with a stainless steel prep island, and a new playground.

The anniversary weekend in September ran from Friday to Sunday. Friday was a cocktail social. Saturday was the Championship Schwingfest, with a steak and Älplermagronen dinner and dancing afterwards. Sunday opened with Mass, then the children's Schwingfest, Steinstossen and a parade.

What the club does

  • Runs three Schwingfests a year and a weekly wrestling practice.
  • Keeps a chorus, the Swiss Echoes, going since 1947.
  • Holds Jass parties, Oktoberfest, a children's Christmas party and a members' BBQ.
  • Marches in the Ripon Almond Blossom Parade each February.
  • Runs a Swiss camp for children each summer.
  • Rents its hall to the public.
