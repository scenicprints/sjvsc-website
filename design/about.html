1926 – 2026

About the club

A hundred years of Swiss life in the San Joaquin Valley, run by its members.

How it started

Swiss families came to the San Joaquin Valley to farm, most of them dairymen. The San Joaquin Valley Swiss Club was founded in 1926 so they had somewhere to meet, wrestle, sing and keep the language. It was incorporated in 1941.

The club is a non-profit organisation, owned and run by its members.

The hall

The hall sits on Manley Road at the edge of Ripon. The carved sign over the door gives both dates, 1926 and 1941. Inside, the cantonal crests are mounted along the wall above the dance floor.

It is used for club events most months of the year, and rented out the rest of the time. Rental income keeps the building standing.

One hundred years

The club turned one hundred in 2026. Members spent that year working on the building. They replaced the old entrance, refinished the dance floor, rebuilt the kitchen and put in a playground.

The anniversary weekend in September ran three days. A cocktail social on the Friday. The Championship Schwingfest on the Saturday, with a steak and Älplermagronen dinner and dancing after. On the Sunday, Mass, the children's Schwingfest, Steinstossen and a parade.

What the club does

  • Runs three Schwingfests a year and a weekly wrestling practice.
  • Keeps a 32-voice chorus, the Swiss Echoes, going since 1947.
  • Holds Jass parties, Oktoberfest, a children's Christmas party and a members' BBQ.
  • Marches in the Ripon Almond Blossom Parade each February.
  • Runs a Swiss camp for children each summer.
  • Rents its hall to the public.

Fundraising

The centennial cookbook is $35. Engraved bricks for the new playground are $150 and take three lines of twenty characters.
