Three a year — June, July and September

Schwingfest

Swiss wrestling in a ring of sawdust, held under the West Coast Swiss Wrestling Association.

The three Schwingfests

The club holds three a year. Dates for the year ahead are set at the Annual General Meeting each December.

TournamentNextWho wrestles
Spring SchwingfestSat 5 June 2027Seniors and juniors
Buebe & Meitli SchwingfestSat 24 July 2027Children up to 15
Championship SchwingfestSat 4 September 2027Seniors and juniors, 16 and over

Coming to watch

Sign-ups are in the morning and wrestling starts late morning, running most of the day. The counter opens for lunch and the bar is open through the afternoon.

The Buebe & Meitli Schwingfest in July is held with the Members' BBQ, where members and their children under 18 eat free.

Wrestling

Practice is Thursday evenings at six. Beginners and children are welcome and the club has breeches to lend. Call Don Widmer on (209) 915-4245 before your first night.
