Children's Christmas Party

Samichlaus comes to the hall for the children and grandchildren of members.

Samichlaus

In Switzerland, Samichlaus comes on 6 December carrying a sack of nuts, mandarins, gingerbread and chocolate. Children who meet him recite a verse or sing before he hands the sack over. The club has kept the custom since the beginning.

On the day

The party runs in the afternoon at the hall. There is food for the children and coffee for everyone else.

Tell the secretary how many children you are bringing and their ages, so that Samichlaus arrives with the right number of sacks.
