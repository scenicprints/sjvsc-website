Swiss wrestling

Schwingen

The national sport of Switzerland. The club has wrestled since 1926 and holds three tournaments a year.

What it is

Schwingen is a form of wrestling from the Swiss Alps. Wrestlers pull short breeches of heavy burlap on over their trousers. Each man takes a grip on the other's breeches, and from that grip tries to put his opponent down.

A fall counts when both shoulder blades touch the ground and the winner still has a hand on the breeches. Bouts are wrestled in a circular ring of sawdust, outdoors, and last about five minutes. There are no weight classes.

Three judges score each bout out of ten. The two highest-scoring wrestlers of the day meet in a final bout called the Schlussgang. At a federal tournament the overall winner is named Schwingerkönig.

Tournaments

The club runs three Schwingfests a year under the West Coast Swiss Wrestling Association.
