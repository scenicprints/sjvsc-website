Scholarships

Each year the San Joaquin Valley Swiss Club awards scholarships to direct descendants of SJVSC members.

Who can apply

Applicants must have a parent or grandparent who has been a member in good standing for the last three years.

Applications are due the last day in February for the scholarship year.

Winners

Jakob Bouma, Natalie Guintoli, Kathryn Kuttel, Victor Guizar, Clayton Durrer and Silvia Schallberger.

Congratulations to these young Swiss individuals for their academic achievements. We wish you the very best in your college years and in your future, and look forward to seeing you at the Swiss Club.

How they are funded

These scholarships have been made possible by generous supporters of the Oktoberfest and the San Joaquin Valley Swiss Club, to encourage students to pursue higher learning.

In addition to the memorial fund, we would like to remind members that donations can be made directly to the San Joaquin Valley Swiss Club Scholarship Fund.
