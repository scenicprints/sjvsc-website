Swiss wrestling

Schwingfests

The club holds three Schwingfests a year, under the West Coast Swiss Wrestling Association.

What is Schwingen?

Swiss wrestling, also known as Schwingen, is Switzerland’s national sport. It is a traditional form of wrestling practised on a circular ground covered with sawdust.

Natively it is called Hoselupf — breeches-lifting. Wrestlers wear Schwingerhosen, heavy wrestling breeches with belts that are used for taking holds. Throws and trips are common, because the first to pin the other’s shoulders to the ground wins the bout.

The three Schwingfests

  • The Spring Schwingfest — seniors and juniors.
  • The Buebe and Meitli Schwingfest — for the children, held with the Members’ BBQ and the Swiss National Day celebration.
  • The Championship Schwingfest — the big one of the year.

Coming to watch

Sign-ups are in the morning and wrestling starts late morning, running most of the day. The counter opens for lunch and the bar is open through the afternoon. Admission is $20 for anyone 15 and over.

Wrestling practice

Practice is at the club hall on Thursdays at 6pm. Beginners and children are welcome and the club has breeches to lend. Call Don for more info on (209) 915-4245.
