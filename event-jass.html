The Swiss card game

Jass

The club holds two Jass parties a year. Beginners are welcome — there is a learning table.

How it works at the Swiss Club

Men play on one side of the table and women on the other. After each hand one side moves down a space, which makes changing partners easy, so you play with new people every round.

Nine hands are played before lunch. After lunch the opposite side moves.

The hall opens at noon. If you do not know how to play, or want to refamiliarise yourself with the game, come at noon for the learning table.

The day

A light lunch is served between rounds, and there is a raffle and door prizes.

The cost is $15, which covers both the Jass and the lunch.

Learning

American Jass can be played four or six handed. The club has a cheat sheet and a fuller guide to how the play works here.

There are also Swiss Jass apps for your phone. Some use Jass cards, and some of the rules differ from the way the game is played at the club — Jass at the Swiss Club is the gentler version.
