Almond Blossom Parade and Dance

The City of Ripon hosts the annual parade, and the Swiss Club has a float in it. Come join the fun and ride on the parade.

The parade

The City of Ripon hosts the annual Almond Blossom Parade, and the Swiss Club enters a float. Anyone who wants to ride on it is welcome.

The dance

The Almond Blossom Dance is from 7pm to 11pm at the Ripon Swiss Hall. Wristbands are $10, cash only.

You must be 21 or over, so make sure you have your ID ready. Food and drink are available for purchase.

Helping out

Volunteers are needed to help with the float, selling wristbands at the door, scrip, set up and clean up. Free entrance for helping out.

Please call or text Regina Beeler on (209) 484-4961. Hope to see you there.
