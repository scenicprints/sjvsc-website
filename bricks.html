The playground walk

A brick with your name on it

The playground beside the hall is being paid for brick by brick. Each engraved brick is laid in the walk beside it, with three lines of your own, for $150.

What it is

The new playground was finished in October 2025, and the club is still paying for it. An engraved brick in the walk beside it is how you help: a family name, a name to remember someone by, a date, a few words. Three lines, up to twenty characters each, spaces included.

A brick is $150.

See it before you order

Type your three lines and the brick below shows them as they would be cut. Capitals only, the way bricks are engraved. When they are right, the button opens an email to the club with the wording in it, and the club will tell you how to pay.
