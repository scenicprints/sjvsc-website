Schwinger’s Club

The Schwinger’s Club promotes Schwingen as the cultural heritage the Swiss brought to America.

What it does

The Schwinger’s Club is an auxiliary of the San Joaquin Valley Swiss Club. It donates prizes and money to the Schwingfests held at the club.

The Schwinger Dinner

Each year the Schwinger’s Club puts on a dinner to a sold-out crowd, usually the second Saturday in March. About 300 people come for prime rib, shrimp and Älplermagronen.

There are raffle prizes, a silent dessert auction and the gun auction.

Tickets go on sale in January and sell out fast.
